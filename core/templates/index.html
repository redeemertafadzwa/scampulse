Privacy

Your messages stay on your phone. The scam check runs entirely on your device. Nothing is uploaded when you check a message.

You only share something if you tap Report as scam — and we redact phone numbers, links, amounts, account numbers and names first, and show you exactly what will be sent before anything leaves your phone.

We store an anonymous random device token (for rate limits and one-vote-per-device) — only a hash of it is kept on the server. No accounts, no names, no sign-up.

Tap “Delete my data” in Settings to clear everything stored on this device.
