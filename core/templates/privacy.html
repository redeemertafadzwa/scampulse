Privacy

Your messages stay on your phone. The scam check runs entirely on your device using a small model downloaded once. Nothing is uploaded when you check a message.

Reporting is opt-in. You only share something if you tap Report as scam. Before anything leaves your phone we redact phone numbers (+263 and 07x), links, amounts, account numbers and names, and show you exactly what will be sent. Nothing uploads automatically.

No accounts. We store an anonymous random device token, used only for rate limits and one-vote-per-device. The server keeps only a hash of it — never names, emails, or sign-ups.

You are in control. “Delete my data” in Settings clears the device token, safe-word, local blocklist and any queued reports from this phone.

What we never claim. ScamPulse cannot check audio or video and never claims to detect deepfakes. It can miss things — if money or your PIN is involved, always confirm with the real company.
